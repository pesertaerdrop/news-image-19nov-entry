**Thursday 19 November 2026: the world in one image — 36 stories, three leaders, one debut** — 36 stories, 7 regions, one page. Thirteen illustrated lead stories (the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen, GTA VI's release after 13 years, Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO debut, the World Court's Myanmar ruling, COP31's final 48 hours, Cyclone Akvan, Ukraine watching Shenzhen, the draft Shenzhen Declaration, Florida's second day of floods, the Kerch Bridge hit again, Banxico's split minutes, the India–Japan 2+2, and allies meeting in Berlin), a world map of where it happened, 23 more briefs, a six-number stat strip and a date check of what was deliberately left out. Every story happened, was first reported, or is due on Thursday 19 November 2026. All thirteen illustrations were generated from structured prompts built with prompts.chat (`npx prompts.chat`); the page itself is HTML/CSS rendered to a 1024×1024 PNG, and the program simply copies that image to the output.
